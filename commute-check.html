Commute Check Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Location Data: When you submit a service report, we collect your approximate location to verify you are near a train station. Location is only accessed when you actively submit a report and with your explicit permission.

Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address for authentication purposes.

Usage Data: We collect anonymous statistics about which features of the app are used, for example that a timetable was opened or a route was saved. They are tied to a random ID for the app install, never to your account, email address or location.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify report authenticity through location proximity
  • To authenticate your account
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To send important service announcements (optional)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Location data is not stored permanently - it is only used at the time of report submission for verification purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for crash reporting, analytics and push notifications. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

We use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, for the anonymous usage statistics described above. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account
  • Location data is not stored after report verification
  • Anonymous usage analytics are retained indefinitely

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Commute Check is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us through the app or via the Play Store listing.

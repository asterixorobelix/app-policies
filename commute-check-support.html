Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view train schedules?

Open the app and select your departure station from the station list. You'll see the next departing trains with their destination and current status. Schedules automatically adjust for weekdays, Saturdays, and Sundays.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No account is required to view train schedules and read service reports. You only need to create an account if you want to submit your own reports to help fellow commuters.

How do service reports work?

Commuters at stations and on trains can submit real-time reports about delays, cancellations, crowding, or on-time service. Other users can confirm or dispute these reports. The more confirmations a report has, the more reliable it is.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Train schedules are downloaded and cached on your device for offline access. Service reports require an internet connection since they are crowdsourced in real-time.

How do I save my daily commute?

Go to the "My Commutes" screen and tap "+ Add New Commute." Select your departure and arrival stations, give it a name (e.g., "Morning Commute"), and save. Your saved commutes appear on the home screen for quick access.

Which stations are covered?

Commute Check covers all 27 stations on Cape Town's Metrorail Southern Line: Cape Town, Woodstock, Salt River, Observatory, Mowbray, Rosebank, Rondebosch, Newlands, Claremont, Harfield Road, Kenilworth, Wynberg, Wittebome, Plumstead, Steurhof, Diep River, Heathfield, Retreat, Steenberg, Lakeside, False Bay, Muizenberg, St James, Kalk Bay, Fish Hoek, Sunny Cove, and Simon's Town.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and all associated data from the app's settings, or visit our account deletion page.

How is my data used?

We collect minimal data. Location is used only when submitting reports to verify you're near a station. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

The app isn't showing any schedules

Try pulling down on the schedule screen to refresh. If the issue persists, check your internet connection for the initial schedule download. Once downloaded, schedules are available offline.

I can't submit a service report

You need to be signed in to submit reports. Check that you're logged in and have an active internet connection. Reports also require location access to verify you're near a station.

The app crashed or is not responding

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, update to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play Store. If you continue to experience issues, email us with details about what you were doing when the crash occurred.

Still need help? Contact us at commutecheck.app@gmail.com with a description of your issue. Please include your device model and app version.
